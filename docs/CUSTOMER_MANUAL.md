# User Manual - UrbanCart Customer

## Getting Started

### Registration
1. Click "Sign Up" on homepage
2. Enter:
   - First Name
   - Last Name
   - Email
   - Phone Number
   - Password (min 6 characters)
3. Accept Terms & Conditions
4. Click "Create Account"
5. Verify your email (link sent to inbox)
6. Phone verification (SMS code)

### Login
1. Click "Login"
2. Enter email and password
3. Click "Sign In"

### Password Reset
1. Click "Forgot Password"
2. Enter your email
3. Click link in reset email
4. Enter new password
5. Confirm new password

## Shopping

### Browsing Products

**Search**
1. Enter keywords in search bar
2. Press Enter or click search icon
3. View results
4. Refine with filters

**Categories**
1. Click category from menu
2. Browse products
3. View subcategories if available
4. Sort by: Newest, Price, Rating

**Filters**
- Price range
- Ratings
- In stock only
- New arrivals
- Featured

### Product Details

1. Click product image
2. View details:
   - Full description
   - Specifications
   - Available sizes/colors
   - Stock status
   - Customer reviews
   - Rating with stars
3. Related products shown below

### Product Reviews

**Read Reviews**
1. Scroll to reviews section
2. Filter by rating
3. Sort by helpful/recent

**Write Review**
1. Purchase product first
2. Go to order
3. Click "Write Review"
4. Rate 1-5 stars
5. Add title and comment
6. Upload photos (optional)
7. Submit

### Wishlist

**Add to Wishlist**
1. Click heart icon on product
2. Product saved to wishlist

**View Wishlist**
1. Click "Account" → "Wishlist"
2. View all saved products
3. Move to cart or remove

## Shopping Cart

### Add to Cart
1. Select product variant (size, color)
2. Enter quantity
3. Click "Add to Cart"
4. Notification confirms addition

### View Cart
1. Click cart icon (top right)
2. See all items:
   - Product name & image
   - Price and quantity
   - Subtotal
3. Edit quantity or remove items

### Update Quantity
1. Click + or - button
2. Price updates automatically
3. Or enter quantity directly

### Remove from Cart
1. Click "Remove" or trash icon
2. Confirm removal
3. Item deleted from cart

## Checkout

### Step 1: Delivery Address

**Select Saved Address**
1. Click address from list
2. It's now selected

**Add New Address**
1. Click "Add New Address"
2. Enter:
   - Full Name
   - Phone Number
   - Street Address
   - City
   - State
   - Postal Code
   - Country
3. Mark as default (optional)
4. Save

### Step 2: Delivery Slot

1. Select delivery date
2. Choose time slot:
   - Morning (9AM-2PM)
   - Afternoon (2PM-6PM)
   - Evening (6PM-9PM)
3. Confirm slot

### Step 3: Payment Method

**Cash on Delivery (COD)**
1. Select COD option
2. Pay when order arrives
3. Cash or online payment accepted

**Card Payment**
1. Select Card option
2. Enter card details:
   - Card number
   - Expiry date
   - CVV
3. Enter billing address
4. Proceed to confirmation

### Step 4: Order Summary

Review:
- Items and quantities
- Subtotal
- Taxes
- Delivery charge
- Total amount
- Payment method
- Delivery address

### Step 5: Place Order

1. Verify all details
2. Click "Place Order"
3. Get order confirmation
4. Order number emailed to you

## Order Management

### View Orders

1. Click "Account" → "Orders"
2. See all orders with status
3. Most recent first

### Track Order

1. Click "Track" on order
2. View timeline:
   - Ordered
   - Confirmed
   - Processing
   - Shipped
   - Out for Delivery
   - Delivered
3. See current location
4. Delivery agent info (when assigned)
5. Estimated arrival time

### Return Order

**Request Return** (within 30 days)
1. Go to order
2. Click "Request Return"
3. Select reason:
   - Defective
   - Wrong item
   - Changed mind
   - Other
4. Add comments
5. Upload photos
6. Submit

**Return Status**
- Returns request reviewed in 24 hours
- Approved returns get pickup scheduled
- Refund processed within 5-7 days

### Cancel Order

**Before Shipping**
1. Open order
2. Click "Cancel Order"
3. Reason (optional)
4. Confirm cancellation
5. Money refunded to original payment method

## Troubleshooting

### Can't Log In
- Check email and password
- Verify caps lock is off
- Clear browser cache
- Use "Forgot Password" if needed

### Order Not Placed
- Check internet connection
- Verify payment method
- Try different browser
- Contact support

### Delivery Delayed
- Check order tracking
- Weather or traffic delays possible
- Contact support if 24+ hours late

### Product Issue
- Request return within 30 days
- Include photos
- Describe issue
- Get resolution in 24 hours
